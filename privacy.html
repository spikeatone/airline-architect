Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Short version: Airline Architect does not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites. The app does send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, and nothing in them can identify you. Your saved games stay on your device and in your own iCloud.

Information we collect

No personal information. The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifiers, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites. There is no advertising SDK in Airline Architect, so you will never see a tracking permission prompt.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how far players get, and whether the game is too hard, too slow, or asks to be paid for too early, the app sends a small number of anonymous events using TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. They contain counts and fixed categories only:

  • that a new airline was started, and which region it starts in (never its name);
  • that a route was opened or a hub established, with how many routes you have and which game day it is (never which airports);
  • that the upgrade screen was shown, what prompted it and how many aircraft and routes you had, and whether it was closed or the game was unlocked (never a price);
  • whether you chose to get return reminders, and which kind of reminder you opened;
  • that the app asked iOS to show the App Store rating prompt (never whether or how you rated);
  • roughly how many minutes the app had been open.

With each event, TelemetryDeck also receives standard technical details: the app version, the iOS version, the device model, and your device's language and region settings.

Crash and performance reports

When the app crashes, freezes, or is slow to launch, iOS gives it a diagnostic report the next time it opens. The app sends a short summary of that report through TelemetryDeck so we can find and fix the problem: the kind of crash, which part of the app's own code was running, which step of the game was in progress (for example, saving), the app and iOS versions, and whether the device is an iPhone or an iPad. It contains no game content and nothing about you.

What is never sent: your airline's name, its livery, the airports and routes you choose, your aircraft or tail numbers, your finances, your saved games, or anything else you create in the game.

TelemetryDeck is designed to be anonymous: it does not receive a name, an email address, or an advertising identifier, and it replaces the device's identifier with a one-way hash that cannot be traced back to you and is not shared with anyone. On the App Store this is declared as Usage Data (product interaction) and Diagnostics (crash data and performance data), not linked to your identity and not used for tracking.

Your game data

Saved games are stored locally on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, they also sync through Apple's iCloud service so you can continue a game across your own devices. That data is transmitted between your devices and Apple's servers — it never reaches us, and we cannot read, recover, or delete it. It is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

If you are signed into Game Center, the achievements you earn and your leaderboard scores (how fast your airline reaches $100 million, and its net worth on game day 365) are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile, where your friends may see them. This is handled by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy; we only see the same leaderboards anyone can see.

Reminders, ratings and feedback

If you choose to get return reminders, they are scheduled as notifications on your device. Their text, which can include your airline's name, is created on your device and never sent to us. You can turn them off in the app (Alerts › settings) or in iOS Settings. The rating prompt is Apple's own; we never see your rating except as a public App Store review. “Send feedback” opens your email app, and we receive only what you choose to send.

Purchases

Airline Architect offers an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full game. Players who subscribed under an earlier version keep their subscription until they cancel it. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never see or handle your payment details. We use RevenueCat to verify the purchase, which receives an anonymous identifier and the purchase receipt from Apple in order to tell the app whether the game is unlocked. It does not receive personal information from us.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect any. Three third parties are involved: Apple, which processes all payments and provides iCloud and Game Center; RevenueCat, which verifies the purchase; and TelemetryDeck, which receives the anonymous usage statistics and crash reports described above.
